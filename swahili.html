About Healthy Swahili Dishes


Inspired by the rich food culture of the Swahili Coast, we introduce rare dishes from Kenya, Tanzania, Zanzibar and surrounding regions to Gent, Belgium. Our cooking events celebrate culture, healthy eating, and community through carefully prepared meals.
